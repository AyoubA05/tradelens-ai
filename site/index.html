FAQ

Fair questions.

Is this a signal service?

No. TradeLens is a post-trade reflection journal. It analyzes trades you've already taken and never generates trade ideas, predictions, or signals.

Which markets and instruments does it support?

Any market you journal — futures, forex, indices, equities. The workflow is built around SMC/ICT day-trading concepts like killzones, sessions, and setup tagging.

Does the AI see my broker account?

No. There is no broker connection. You log trades and upload chart screenshots yourself; TradeLens only sees what you give it.

What do I need to get started?

A browser and your recent trades. Log your first trade with a chart screenshot and the journal starts building your history from there.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Your entries belong to you and are used only to generate your own reviews and analytics. Never shared, never pooled.

Does it understand SMC/ICT concepts?

Yes. Killzones, sessions, liquidity sweeps, order blocks, and FVG setups are first-class fields in the journal and in every AI review.
