# TradeLens AI — 90-Second Loom Script

A tight, recruiter-facing walkthrough. Record in DEMO_MODE so every screen is
full of data and no API calls are made. Speak to the *why*, not just the clicks.

---

**0:00–0:10 — Hook**
> "Most trading journals track your P&L. TradeLens AI tracks *why* you win and lose."

Open on the dashboard: glass KPI cards, equity curve, Trade of the Week. Let it
breathe for a beat — the point is that this looks like a performance lab, not a
spreadsheet.

**0:10–0:25 — Log a trade**
> "I log a trade the way an SMC trader actually thinks — not just price in, price out."

Go to New Trade. Show the SMC/ICT fields: HTF bias, setup, confirmation model,
liquidity sweep / FVG / order block. Set the entry time and call out the
**killzone auto-filling** from it. Save.

**0:25–0:40 — AI vision review + grading**
> "The AI reads the chart and grades my *process* — against my own strategy rules, not a generic checklist."

Open Trade Detail. Run the screenshot analysis; show the proposed SMC labels.
Then show the **grade chip** (e.g. B+) and expand the **rubric** — five
dimensions, each scored with a note.

**0:40–0:55 — Calendar + killzone analytics**
> "Now the patterns. When do I actually perform?"

Open Analytics / Calendar. Show the killzone performance strip and compare
**NY AM vs London** win rates. Point at Total Edge Leak: "this is what breaking
my rules costs me."

**0:55–1:10 — Weekly AI review**
> "Once a week, it writes the retro I'd never write myself."

Open Weekly Review. Scroll the **5-section report** (What Worked, What Didn't,
Killzone Review, Rule Adherence, Focus for Next Week). Open the **"How the AI
reasoned"** expander to show the thinking summary.

**1:10–1:25 — AI Partner Mode**
> "And if I want to push back, I can just ask."

Open AI Partner. Ask one follow-up — e.g. "was my entry too early?" — and show
the reply coming back in **precise SMC/ICT language** (BOS, FVG fill, OB retest),
scoped to the completed trade. No signals, by design.

**1:25–1:30 — Close**
> "And it remembers — correction memory means the AI has already learned N things about my style."

Cut to the sidebar badge: "AI has learned N corrections." End on the dashboard.
